What is AdZen?

AdZen reimagines how advertisements are discovered. Instead of intrusive popups or banner ads, users browse a clean, scrollable feed of advertisements — similar to how you browse Instagram or Twitter — and find things they're actually interested in.
Businesses and individuals post ads with images and external links. Viewers browse, filter, and engage with ads naturally.

Features:

Browsable Ad Feed — scroll through ads like a social media feed
Smart Filters — sort by most liked, most viewed, newest, or by category
Post Advertisements — upload image, add description, external link, and category
Like & View Tracking — real-time engagement metrics on every ad
Category Browse — filter ads by topic (jobs, products, services, events, etc.)
Responsive UI — works cleanly on mobile and desktop
